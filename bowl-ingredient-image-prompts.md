# Bowl ingredient images: prompts

72 images: one for every item a Soldier can pick on the bowl page and in Pitch a Bowl. They are made to sit on
the app's black page the way Chipotle's sit on white: one small pile of food, nothing else in the frame.

## How to use

1. Paste the **style prompt** into the image maker once (or at the start of every request, if it does not
   remember).
2. Add one **subject line** from the tables below. Make one image per line.
3. Save each image with the exact **Save as** filename (lowercase, hyphens, `.png` or `.jpg`).
4. Send the images back. I will shrink them, add them to the app, and show each one beside its item on both pages.

If an image comes back with a plate, a table or text, regenerate it. Mismatched images make the list look messy.

## Style prompt (use for every image)

> Studio food photograph of a single ingredient for a restaurant ordering app. A small, loose pile of the ingredient, seen from a 45-degree overhead angle, centered, filling about 70% of the frame, with generous empty space around it. Isolated on a pure solid black background (#000000), with no plate, bowl, board, table, utensils, garnish, text or props. Soft, even studio light from above; realistic color and texture; crisp focus on the whole pile. Square 1:1 image, 1024 x 1024 pixels. Same camera angle, lighting, scale and black background for every image in the set.

For the sauces, the subject line already asks for a small white ramekin. That is the one exception to "no dish",
the same as Chipotle shows its salsas.
